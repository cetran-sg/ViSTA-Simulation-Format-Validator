Articulated
Vehicle Mode

Articulated mode expects VUT_status to include Trailer_pos_lat, Trailer_pos_lng, Trailer_heading and all other Trailer_* columns. The trailer is visualised as a separate orange OBB on the trajectory map.

Data Format Guide
ZIP structure
{TestCaseId}/{runId}/VUT_status.csv
{TestCaseId}/{runId}/Environment_actors_true.csv
VUT_status required columns
Time, Step_number, VUT_pos_lat, VUT_pos_lng, VUT_heading, VUT_vel_abs
VUT_status additional columns (Articulated mode)
Trailer_pos_lat, Trailer_pos_lng, Trailer_pos_z, Trailer_heading, Trailer_yaw_rate,
Trailer_jerk_lat, Trailer_jerk_lng, Trailer_accl_lat, Trailer_accl_lng,
Trailer_vel_abs, Trailer_travelled
Environment_actors_true required columns
Time, Step_number, Actor_Id, Actor_type, Actor_pos_true_lat, Actor_pos_true_lng, Actor_heading_true
Coordinate ranges

Latitude: −90 to 90  ·  Longitude: −180 to 360  ·  Heading: −360 to 360  ·  Velocity (VUT_vel_abs): ≥ 0

Vehicle Mode
VUT Dimensions

Dimensions of the Vehicle Under Test used for bounding-box rendering on the trajectory map.

Centre of Gravity (CoG)

Distance from the CoG to the front bumper and left edge. Many simulation tools report positions at the CoG — these offsets anchor the bounding box correctly. Set independently of the overall dimensions, as the CoG may not be at the geometric centre.

Tractor Dimensions

Dimensions of the powered tractor unit. The tractor position is taken from VUT_pos_lat / VUT_pos_lng in VUT_status.

Tractor CoG Offset
Trailer Dimensions

Dimensions of the trailer unit. The trailer position is taken from the Trailer_pos_lat / Trailer_pos_lng columns in VUT_status.

Trailer CoG Offset
Trailer data detected
The uploaded files contain Trailer_* data columns. Switching to Articulated mode will re-validate all runs against the full Trailer_* column requirements and visualise the trailer unit on the map.
